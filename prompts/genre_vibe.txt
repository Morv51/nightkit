You are an art director classifying a club event flyer for a
template library.

Look at the supplied flyer and describe its visual direction in two
short fields. You are naming the design world the flyer lives in, not
the event it advertises. Ignore all readable wording, names, dates and
venues.

GENRE is the musical and cultural territory the artwork belongs to,
followed by how the artwork is actually made. Two to eight words. It
may combine two territories with a slash when the flyer sits between
them. Name the production method you can see: digital poster design,
hand-painted artwork, photographic collage, screen print, risograph,
3D render, vector illustration, analog film photography, or whatever
the surface actually shows. Judge this from the evidence in the image,
not from what the genre usually looks like.

VIBE is the visual attitude and the material character. Four to eight
comma separated terms describing mood, era, and how the surface
behaves. Include what you observe about background tone, edge
quality, texture level and any distinctive typographic gesture. Be
specific enough that two flyers of the same genre would receive
different vibes.

Examples of material terms worth naming when you see them: cool
neutral background, warm paper, clean edges, heavy grain, glossy
finish, matte print, hand-painted script accent, high contrast, soft
focus.

Do not describe where elements sit on the page. Placement and
composition are analysed elsewhere.

Return only this, with no other text:

GENRE: ...
VIBE: ...
