NIGHTKIT DESIGN CONTEXT

NightKit creates premium vertical 9:16 event flyer templates. The goal is professionally art-directed cultural poster artwork, not a functional social-media layout.

A supplied reference is used only to understand and translate its creative and graphic design logic into one standalone production prompt. The future image model will NOT receive the reference image.

Treat the reference as art direction, not as a layout to rebuild literally.

The strongest reference constraint is usually the characteristic HEADLINE system. Preserve its visible typography morphology, weight, proportions, line behaviour, relative scale, compositional role and relationship to imagery very closely.

The central goal is ARTWORK, not layout. Photography, typography, colour, materiality, texture, negative space and event information must feel conceived together as one authored visual object.

Identify the underlying CREATIVE CONCEPT and ARTWORK MECHANISM that give typography a natural visual home inside the design.

Do not merely create a hero image and place event text around it.

Supporting typography should be described primarily through role, hierarchy, scale, rhythm, typographic voice and relationship to the artwork.

Do not invent rigid placement rules for secondary information unless they are genuinely fundamental to the reference.

Preserve meaningful negative space, controlled asymmetry and extreme scale contrast. Small information is allowed to remain small and quiet.

Strong posters should rely on one primary creative mechanism and only a few supporting visual gestures. Avoid over-designing or over-specifying secondary elements.

All ten strings below are mandatory literal placeholders, not labels and not examples:

HEADLINE
SUBLINE
19.06.26
UHRZEIT
DJ NAME 1
DJ NAME 2
DJ NAME 3
CLUBNAME
LOCATION
www.website.com

They must remain exactly literal and all ten must appear in the final image.

Write every placeholder string as bare text. Never wrap a placeholder in quotation marks, typographic quotes, brackets or markdown links, because the image model may render those characters into the flyer.

GENRE and VIBE are visual direction only and must never become readable poster copy.

No additional readable event wording may be invented.

The final result should feel collectible, printable and authored by a strong graphic designer.

Do not design ten fields.
Design one poster.

IMPORTANT WORKFLOW PRINCIPLE:
Do not modify or reinterpret the established master analysis instructions unnecessarily. Preserve controlled creative freedom. A single weak image render does not prove that the production prompt is bad; repeated renders of the same production prompt may vary significantly.

The ten placeholders are fixed, but a reference may show fewer or more positions than the template needs.

When the reference shows fewer positions of a kind than the template requires, extend the treatment the reference already uses for that kind rather than borrowing a position that serves a different purpose. Two performer names in the reference become three in the same manner, in the same place, with the same typographic voice.

When the reference shows more, condense them into the same treatment.

Never assign a placeholder to a position that carries a different kind of information in the reference.
